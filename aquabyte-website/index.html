INTRODUCING AQUABYTE

A smarter way to manage the moving parts of shrimp farming.

Feeding, observing feeding activity, checking water conditions and planning cultivation are all important farm activities. Managing them separately can be challenging.

AquaByte is designed to connect these activities through an automated feeding system, monitoring technologies and a dedicated farmer mobile platform.

HOW IT WORKS

From farm setup to
informed decisions.

A connected process links the farmer, monitoring systems, control architecture and mechanical feeding components.

01

Configure

Enter cultivation information and relevant farm details.

02

Select zones

03

Monitor

04

Process
